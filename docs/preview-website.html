Our Story

A Saree Is Not Just Clothing.
It Is Memory.

Vedajanani Vastralayam was born from a mother's love — and a promise to bring honest, beautiful sarees to every home.

“My mother taught me: when a woman drapes a saree, she carries her family's grace with her.”
— Founder's words, the soul of Vedajanani
The Beginning

From a small shop in Anantapur to homes across India

We started with a simple dream: to sell sarees the way our grandmothers bought them — with trust, touch and honest advice. No inflated prices. No compromise on weave. Just beautiful fabrics, fairly priced.

Today, Vedajanani Vastralayam brings together Cotton, Silk, Chiffon and Georgette sarees from master weavers — each one hand-checked before it reaches you.
