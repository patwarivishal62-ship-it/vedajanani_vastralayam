## 4. How to Showcase & Sell More (Sales Playbook)

### Homepage Flow (built in demo)
1. **Hero** — one emotional headline + 2 CTAs max + social proof stats (500+ customers, 4.8★)
2. **Trust Bar** — Handwoven · Premium · 7-Day Exchange · COD (kills hesitation instantly)
3. **Shop by Collection** — 4 fabric cards (visual, tappable)
4. **Shop by Price** — 3 tiers (budget clarity = more clicks)
5. **Shop by Occasion** — Daily / Office / Festive / Brides / Bridesmaids / Guests
6. **Best Sellers** — proven products first
7. **Brand Story Split** — soul + trust ("Direct from weavers, honest pricing")
8. **New Arrivals** — freshness
9. **Testimonials** — real names + cities
10. **Blog Preview** — authority + SEO
11. **Instagram "Worn by You"** — UGC = trust
12. **Newsletter** — 10% off first order (email list = repeat sales)

### Product Page Conversion Elements
- 4–6 images (front, drape, blouse, texture close-up) + video
- Colour variants, honest price + compare-at (SAVE % badge)
- Ratings + reviews (Judge.me) near the top
- Fabric, length, blouse, care — all answered upfront (fewer returns)
- Sticky Add-to-Cart on mobile, Buy It Now, trust icons (Free Shipping / Exchange / UPI-COD)
- "You May Also Like" — increases basket size
- Quick Add on collection pages — one-click purchase

### Cart & Checkout
- Cart drawer with free-shipping progress bar (pushes basket above ₹999)
- Guest checkout ON, 1-page checkout, UPI first (India's favourite)
- COD available (huge for saree buyers)
- Thank-you page with order timeline + "Continue Shopping" (repeat purchase)

### Pricing Psychology
- Anchor with compare-at prices (₹1,799 → ₹1,299) — honest, not fake
- Name the tiers ("Daily Grace", "Most Loved", "Heirloom") — makes ₹3,000 feel like an heirloom, not an expense
- Bundle bridesmaid sets (Set of 4 at a special price) — high basket value

### Trust & Retention
- WhatsApp support button (saree buyers love asking about fabric)
- 7-day exchange, clear shipping policy, track order
- Klaviyo emails: Welcome (10% off) → Abandoned cart → Festive preview → Weaver story
- Google Reviews + Instagram UGC reposted on site

## 5. Storytelling — Giving the Business a Soul

### The Core Story (use everywhere)
> *"A saree is not just clothing. It is memory, identity and grace — passed from mother to daughter. Vedajanani Vastralayam was born from a mother's love and a promise: honest fabric, fair prices, timeless design."*

### Story Touchpoints (all built into the demo)
1. **Hero Founder's Note** — personal quote on homepage
2. **Our Story page** — Beginning → Journey timeline (2023–2026) → Values (Honest Weaves, Fair Prices, Weaver First)
3. **Weaver Stories (Blog)** — "Inside the Weaver's Loom" — hands, heritage, patience
4. **Product descriptions** — each saree has a soul line ("Heirloom Banarasi — made to be treasured")
5. **Founder's Picks** — "the sarees I would drape on my own family" — personal endorsement
6. **Testimonials** — real women, real cities, real love
7. **"Worn by You" Instagram** — customers become the story
8. **Thank-you page** — "Thank You, Beautiful!" — warm, personal, memorable

### Voice & Tone
- Warm, respectful, graceful — never loud or discount-shouting
- Words to use: *grace, weave, drape, heirloom, handpicked, honest*
- Words to avoid: *cheap, sale blast, limited stock panic*

## 6. Design System (Top-Notch Look)

| Element | Choice |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Ivory `#F9F6F1` (bg) · Deep Maroon `#5C1A24` (primary) · Antique Gold `#B9975B` (accent) · Charcoal `#1C1C1C` (text/footer) |
| **Headings** | Playfair Display (premium serif) |
| **Body / Product names** | Cormorant Garamond + Jost (wide letter-spacing on labels) |
| **Layout** | 12-col grid, 1280px max, 24px gutters, 96px section padding (48px mobile) |
| **Images** | 3:4 ratio everywhere, ivory backgrounds, texture close-ups |
| **Feel** | Boutique showroom — quiet, warm, spacious. One action per section. |
